How Proof works

From a working session to a living professional record.

Proof connects the work people do together with the reputation they build over time.

01 / COLLABORATE

Work in a shared room

Bring people together for code, design, research, business cases, or structured interviews.

02 / CAPTURE

Keep the working context

Session events and outcomes inform a report, so the reasoning behind the result is easier to review.

03 / BUILD

Make progress visible

Profiles bring together work history, skills, session reports, and professional connections.

One network, two sides

Better context for people and the teams that find them.

Professionals can build a profile around more than a resume. Hiring teams can search profiles, review evidence, compare candidates, and invite people to structured sessions.
