Proof of IntelligenceInvestor overview · 2026
01 / The problem

Hiring sees the profile. It misses the work.

The artifacts used to evaluate people often separate what they claim from how they actually work.

01

Claims are easy to polish

Resumes, portfolios, and credentials describe a person, but rarely show how the work unfolded.

02

Interviews are snapshots

Short conversations compress reasoning, collaboration, and judgment into a narrow window.

03

Context gets discarded

Most hiring records capture the answer, not the decisions, revisions, and teamwork behind it.

The gap: a professional record should carry more than self-reported claims.

07 / Business model

Start with the team workflow. Test the network effect.

Participation loop

Professionals + teams

Test a focused workflow with a small set of recruiting teams before widening the market.
