Company overview

Build trust through work people can review.

Proof is developing a professional network and hiring workflow around demonstrated collaboration. The product is built; the next job is proving repeat use, buyer demand, and a deployable operating model.

Business model direction

Start where evidence changes a hiring decision.

For teams

Hiring and assessment workflows

Company workspaces already support jobs, candidate pipelines, profile search and comparison, and structured assessments. A paid team workspace is the clearest monetization direction to validate with recruiters and employers.

For professionals

A portable record of work

Profiles and session reports give professionals a reason to participate and share their work. That network effect is a hypothesis to test, not a proven acquisition-cost advantage.

Current stage

Working product. Commercial proof still ahead.

In the repository

End-to-end product surface

Profiles, live rooms, session reports, professional networking, jobs, company tools, and an investor demo dataset are implemented in the all-in-one source release.

To validate next

Use, conversion, and deployment

Repeat engagement, willingness to pay, customer acquisition, production readiness, and operating costs need real-world validation before they can be represented as traction.

No revenue, customer, pilot, or growth metrics are presented here because none are substantiated in the source materials reviewed.

Execution priorities

Turn a broad product into a focused operating business.

  • Run focused sessions with hiring teams and professionals; measure whether session evidence changes evaluation decisions.
  • Validate a narrow initial customer and workflow before expanding the product surface.
  • Harden hosting, media, code execution, identity, billing, and email for a public service.
  • Establish baseline product, retention, conversion, and cost metrics before making scale claims.
